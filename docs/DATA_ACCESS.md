| Accession | Source | Role and boundary |
|---|---|---|

| GSE207889 | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE207889 | 24,034 organoid cells, four technical libraries from one differentiation; no sesamin treatment |
| GSE115469 | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE115469 | 8,444 adult-liver atlas cells, five donors; cellular reference, no sesamin intervention |

Raw source data remain at their original repositories. Included processed inputs, numerical results, annotations and mapping information are identified in the versioned supplementary archive and source maps. Original dataset accessions and rights remain applicable. A repository mirror is not a substitute for citing the original study and dataset.

The source study's own Table 2 gives dietary rat physiological context. Such previously published measurements are not new measurements generated here. Existing sample labels and publicly deposited cell annotations are not reclassified as independent experiments.
