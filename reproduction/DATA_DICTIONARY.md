# Input data dictionary and analytical units

`provenance/source_manifest.json` is the authoritative per-file SHA-256 and byte-count inventory. All paths below are relative to `inputs/`. Missing candidate measurements remain NA and are never replaced by zero. Human and mouse values are kept on their own measurement scales; no pooled cross-species effect is calculated.

|---|---|---|

The nine `Control_1..3`, `Model_1..3`, `Treat_1..3` columns contain nonnegative integer counts; 60,605 rows are retained. Other annotation columns are unused. | Re-estimate size factors from all genes positive in each retained-library subset; compute candidate effects, CPM, transcriptome QC. |

## Output fields and estimands

Human/mouse `configuration=0` keeps all nine libraries. Each later configuration retains two or three per group; `enumerated_configurations.csv` lists the exact retained zero-based positions and library IDs. `configuration_size_factors.csv` records species/library factors for every setting. `log2_ratio` uses a +1 pseudocount for normalized counts and CPM, or +0.1 for TPM. `direction` is the sign of the unshifted group difference. `percent_difference_no_pseudocount` is 100 × (numerator−denominator)/denominator and is NA for a zero denominator. Configuration min/max ranges are sensitivity ranges, not intervals for a population parameter.

Rat `effect_log2_signal` is mean sesamin minus mean control after median aggregation over eligible probes. Welch confidence limits and p-values use the same five animals per group. `q_bh_fixed25` uses a family size of 25, temporarily substituting p=1 for unmeasured candidates for multiplicity calculation and restoring their q-values to NA. Exact p-values are the two-sided fraction of all 252 allocations with absolute difference at least the observed value (comparison tolerance 1e-12). BH is again over 25. Both primary and sensitivity normalizations remain separate.

Organoid `detected_pct` is 100 × detected cells / cells in the specified library/category/subtype, including zero-count cells. `mean_log1p_cp10k` is the arithmetic mean of cell-wise log-transformed expression, not log of a group mean. In `equal_library_actual`, each of two libraries within a condition has weight 1/2. Category proportions are averaged across libraries; category expression means use proportion-weighted library means so the category mixture reconstructs the actual equal-library whole-library mean. `pooled_cell_actual` weights cells equally. `separate_average_reference_diagnostic` uses separate unweighted category-expression averages and explicitly reports the library composition-expression covariance needed to recover the actual equal-library contrast.

For each gene and weighting, with condition-specific category fractions p and expression means mu, the symmetric composition term is `(p_PA − p_CTRL) * (mu_PA + mu_CTRL)/2`; the within-category term is `(mu_PA − mu_CTRL) * (p_PA + p_CTRL)/2`. Summing both over the four categories exactly reconstructs the corresponding PA-minus-control mixture mean, within numerical precision. Units are mean log1p(CP10k), not fold changes or causal effect fractions. `kitagawa_terms_all25.csv` retains individual categories, and `kitagawa_summary_all25.csv` retains all 25 candidates × three schemes.
