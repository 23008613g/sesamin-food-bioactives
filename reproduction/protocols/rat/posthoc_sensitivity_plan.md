# Post hoc small-sample model sensitivity

Recorded after the initial Welch results were inspected on 2026-10-09. VNN1 was the only fixed-family BH<0.05 candidate. No candidate or primary analysis is changed.

To assess how the small number of animals affects the test conclusion, enumerate all choose(10,5)=252 allocations of the ten selected control/sesamin animals for every originally mapped candidate, separately for both existing normalizations. Use the absolute difference in group means of the frozen log-transformed probe aggregate as the statistic. The exact tail fraction includes every allocation and the observed allocation, with numerical tolerance 1e-12; apply BH across the same original 25 hypotheses with unavailable hypotheses only assigned 1 for adjustment.

These are exchangeability-based conditional reference P values. Random allocation of treatment was not verified, and body-weight selection was performed by the original investigators; enumeration does not remove these limitations or confer randomization-based causal validity. The finite minimum two-sided P for complete separation can limit multiplicity-adjusted detection. Disagreement with the Welch result must be reported; it is not proof of no effect. No expression or sample is excluded, no smaller candidate family is used, and no result is selected according to significance.
