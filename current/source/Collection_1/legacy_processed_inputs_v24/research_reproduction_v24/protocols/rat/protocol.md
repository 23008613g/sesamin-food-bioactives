# Fixed analysis plan for GSE42693

Created 2026-10-09 before inspecting candidate effect estimates in this dataset. The source matrix had previously been cached and format-audited at stage06; the existing audit explicitly states that no effects were calculated. This plan is recorded locally and is not a registered protocol.

- Question: are the fixed 25 human-screen candidates associated with dietary sesamin exposure in an independent rat liver study? A general liver response is distinct from agreement with a fatty-acid injury/recovery experiment.
- Source: deposited GSE42693 series matrix, GPL1355, 31,099 probes and 20 arrays. Primary control GSM1048036–GSM1048040 and sesamin GSM1048041–GSM1048045, 5 individual rat livers per group, 0.2% dietary sesamin for 15 days. Other two compounds are retained in source/QC only.
- Selection limitation: original investigators chose 5 of 7 animals in each group after excluding highest/lowest sacrifice body weights. We retain all deposited animals. Inference concerns these selected animals; it cannot undo that original selection or establish effects in a disease model.
- Freeze the existing 25 human gene IDs. Obtain official human-to-rat Ensembl orthology without examining expression effects. Primary mapping requires one-to-one orthology and a uniquely resolved rat gene. Use original human IDs; retired/unmapped IDs remain unavailable. Report orthology type and confidence if supplied; do not fabricate confidence thresholds. Map GPL1355 probes through deposited gene identifiers, preferring unique Entrez IDs connected to the rat Ensembl gene. Ambiguous multi-gene probes are excluded by annotation alone and listed. If Entrez mapping is unavailable, any alternative mapping must be documented before candidate effects are inspected.
- Primary expression: log2(deposited globally scaled positive signal + 1), using the source values rather than calling these raw counts or RMA. For multiple uniquely annotated probes per rat gene, take the median log expression within each animal; preserve all contributing probe values and probe-specific direction estimates. No most-significant-probe selection or effect-based expression filter.
- Primary effect: mean log2 signal in sesamin minus control. Report all mapped candidates with per-animal values, group summaries, Welch 95% confidence interval and two-sided Welch t test (unequal variances, independent rat samples). Small-sample normal-on-log-scale approximation and original selection are explicit limitations. No inferred statistical tests in the original human/mouse or one-differentiation organoid datasets.
- Multiple testing: Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment across the original 25-candidate family, using p=1 placeholders solely in adjustment for unavailable/untestable hypotheses, while displayed unavailable estimates/P remain NA. Primary FDR threshold 0.05. Report effect directions and intervals regardless of threshold.
- Sensitivity: deterministic quantile normalization of all 20 log-transformed arrays, then the same fixed probe aggregation and primary contrast; report separately without choosing the better result. Mean-versus-median group summaries and probe direction disagreement are additional descriptive checks. Assess all-array distributions, correlations and PCA for gross sample issues; remove no array based on outcome or PCA.
- Compare signs with the fixed human Treat–Model and mouse sess–HFD results only after rat analysis. Direction agreement is descriptive and does not pool cross-platform magnitudes or establish common mechanism. Retain unavailable orthologues, contradictory effects and low signals in the reporting table.
- Record source hashes, source URLs, library versions, mapping availability, exact commands and failures. No experimental work, installation or external upload.
