Supplementary Figure S19. Signal-rank distribution and normalization stability of existing limma hits. a, Empirical cumulative distributions of the mean-signal percentiles of every gene with limma BH q <0.05 in the deposited (24 genes), QN10 (41) and QN20 (41) analyses. Each gene's percentile is calculated within the complete 14,604-gene mean-log2-signal distribution of the same normalization, using the fraction of gene means less than or equal to its own. The dashed diagonal is a uniform-rank reference, not a fitted null model; the QN10 and QN20 curves are closely overlapping. b, All nonempty intersections of the three hit sets. Twenty-four genes occur in all three lists and 17 occur in both QN lists only; no other hit-membership patterns occur. The union contains 41 genes, all with the same nonzero sesamin-minus-control effect sign across the three analyses. These post-hoc diagnostics neither refit models nor change the tested gene family. Signal ranks are not detection thresholds. All three analyses use the same five control and five sesamin animals; overlap is analytical stability, not independent replication. No new statistical test, confidence interval, expression filter or biological validation is introduced.
