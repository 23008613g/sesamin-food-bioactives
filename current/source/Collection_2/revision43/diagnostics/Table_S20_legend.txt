Supplementary Table S20. Signal-intensity distribution and normalization stability of existing limma BH hits. BH q <0.05 is applied separately to the same 14,604-gene family in each previously executed normalization. Mean log2 signal is the arithmetic mean across the ten primary arrays. Percentiles use the right-continuous empirical distribution of all gene means within each normalization; Q1-Q4 denote (0,25], (25,50], (50,75] and (75,100] percentiles. These exploratory descriptive strata are not detection thresholds or filtering rules. The three analyses use the same animals. The shared hit intersection contains 24 genes; the union contains 41. Complete hit lists, all-gene ranks and effect/interval values are supplied separately.
