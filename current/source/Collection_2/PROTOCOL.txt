Stage 39 exploratory whole-transcriptome analysis
Client date 2026-10-13 Asia/Taipei

Purpose and prior knowledge
Test whether sesamin-associated expression observations extend beyond a candidate family selected in HepG2, using the complete GSE42693 rat matrix. This is a post-hoc exploratory extension. Prior candidate findings including VNN1/AREG and original published rat lipid responses are known. It is not preregistered, blinded, a held-out experiment, or a normal-human-hepatocyte validation. The protocol is frozen before executing the new full-genome effects, gene-set tests and cross-species comparisons. Fixed public annotations and their QC may be inspected before freezing.

Input and experimental units
GSE42693 deposited 31,099 probes x 20 arrays, GCOS 1.4 globally scaled unlogged signals. Primary samples are control GSM1048036-1048040 and sesamin GSM1048041-1048045, five individual male rat livers per group, 0.2% dietary sesamin for 15 days. Episesamin and sesamolin arrays are used only in the predefined 20-array normalization sensitivity, never pooled with sesamin. No new animal exclusions. Original investigators excluded terminal-weight extremes from seven animals per diet; random allocation and exchangeability are not established by our reanalysis. No raw CEL processing and no inference that the deposited matrix reproduces the source GeneSpring workflow.

Gene universe and expression
Match all platform probes by exact probe identifier to the complete GPL1355 annotation dated 2016-08-09. Retain probes with exactly one Entrez identifier; exclude ambiguous/no-ID probes and document counts. Symbols serve only as labels. Summarize all eligible probes for each Entrez gene by the within-array median of log2(signal+1), not by choosing the largest effect. Freeze the gene universe using finite values and positive sample variance across all ten primary animals, independent of labels and HepG2 membership. Do not impose an effect, p-value, GO-membership, candidate or dietary-response filter. Report intensity summaries and probe multiplicity; detection calls unavailable in this matrix do not justify declaring every gene detectably expressed.

Normalization sensitivity
Primary: deposited log2(signal+1). Secondary: exact-tie-aware quantile normalization of log probe signals across the ten primary arrays (qn10) and across all twenty arrays (qn20), before gene median aggregation. Maintain the primary gene testing universe under all three variants. QN is a sensitivity, not proof of biological correction; it can remove true global expression shifts. Store all gene/sample matrices and full results.

Gene inference
For every gene and variant report group means, SD, n, sesamin-minus-control mean log2-signal difference, nominal 95% Welch interval, Welch t/df/two-sided p and BH across all genes, plus BY sensitivity across the same family. Separately enumerate all 252 assignments of five of the ten animals to treatment, including the observed and complementary assignments. Exact p = number(abs(permuted mean difference)>=abs(observed)-tol)/252, tol=1e-12*max(1,abs(observed)). No Monte Carlo +1. Label-independent preprocessing stays fixed. The reference requires exchangeability and does not repair selection bias. For non-estimable zero within-group SE use NA inferential quantities, include p=1 in multiplicity adjustment, and report the case. Constant/zero permutation effects give p=1. No gene-level inference is presented as human validation.

Existing R sensitivity
Use installed R 4.6.1 and limma 3.68.4 (no installation), lmFit with intercept and sesamin indicator, then eBayes(trend=TRUE, robust=FALSE, proportion=0.01, stdev.coef.lim=c(0.1,4)). Analyze the identical gene matrix/universe for each variant. Save every gene with coefficient, moderated SE, t, df, nominal 95% moderated interval and p/BH/BY. Compare to Welch and exact-label results transparently. This is a predefined sensitivity, not a replacement selected after seeing which test is significant.

Biological-process gene sets
Membership is the union of GO:Process IDs listed for eligible probes per Entrez in GPL1355 (2016), without additional ancestor propagation or evidence-code filtering (these fields are unavailable). Freeze official go-basic.obo releases/2026-07-26 (downloaded 2026-10-13 local date; SHA256 b08d45b268b8c24ccb2513dbbbc7d4df9f6521c099b413f79eb31e06e0fa3bcc). Resolve unique alt_id to current canonical IDs, exclude missing, obsolete and non-biological-process IDs, and retain a complete term audit. Do not redirect obsolete terms through replaced_by/consider. This updates term status/names but does not update the historical gene-term associations. Call them platform-annotated GO biological-process gene sets, not a current complete pathway collection. Test every set with 15-500 distinct eligible genes inclusive. Retain each distinct GO ID even if memberships coincide; correct across all tested GO IDs and report identical-set hashes.

Gene-set score and inference
For each variant, standardize each gene over the ten animals using sample SD (ddof=1), then average member z scores equally within each animal. A zero-variance sensitivity gene contributes zero standardized values and remains in the fixed family. Compare the five sample scores per group using Welch and all 252 whole-sample label assignments. These preserve within-set gene correlation. BH and BY are applied over every tested GO ID separately for each variant/test; BY assesses dependence among overlapping sets. Report mean score differences and nominal Welch CIs. These are self-contained coordinated-expression scores; mixed-direction genes can cancel. Do not call the statistic competitive GSEA, pathway activation, flux, or a causal mechanism.

Cross-species comparison
HepG2 full counts: GSE248903_total_count.anno.txt.gz. Explicit order Control_1-3, Model_1-3, Treat_1-3. CPM denominator all 60,605 records; CPM>=1 in >=3 of 9 retains 13,014. Median-ratio reference uses all records positive in nine libraries, geometric recentering. Effects: log2[(mean normalized count+1)/(reference mean+1)], Treat/Model and Model/Control. Reproduce archived full-gene effects and filter before comparison. No human p values due to unresolved independent culture units/timing. Do not apply the 25-candidate rule, stability, significance or fold threshold to the comparison.
Mapping: fixed Ensembl116 high-confidence one-to-one human-rat genes, with human Ensembl, rat Ensembl and rat Entrez all unambiguous in the complete source maps. Intersect with eligible rat genes and retained human genes. Save all included/excluded mappings. Report Spearman correlation descriptively, positive/negative/zero direction contingency and rates for all shared genes, plus rat-normalization and human-CPM sensitivities. No gene-independence p values or binomial intervals. Contrasts differ in species, model, challenge and exposure, so correlation is not validation or proof of rescue. Also compute matched-member GO scores only for gene sets retaining 15-500 shared genes: standardize within each study (rat 10 animals, human 9 libraries), use identical mapped members and show score directions descriptively without human inferential tests.
